2. EDUC — Educational Attainment Recode
Definition: Recoded educational attainment for persons in the CPS universe IPUMS CPS.
Typical code labels (IPUMS CPS):

000 — No schooling (NIU or blank)

001 — None, preschool, or kindergarten

010–014 — Grades 1–4

020–022 — Grades 5–6

030 — Grades 7–8

040–042 — Grades 9–12

050–052 — Grades 13–15 (postsecondary)

060–062 — Grades 16–18 (graduate)

070–072 — Grades 19–21 (postgraduate)

080–082 — Grades 22–24 (doctoral)

090–092 — Grades 25–27 (professional)

100–102 — Grades 28–30 (other)
